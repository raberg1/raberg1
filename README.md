## Robotics - FRC Team 4645 - The ChicagoStyle BotDogs

The Chicago Style Botdogs, officially FRC Team 4645, is a robotics team with a diverse and talented background of 100+ students. From encouraging opportunities in STEM to helping other FRC teams, our team aims to benefit the surrounding communities near Lane Tech and Chicago through mentoring students and promoting FIRST robotics. Our team is almost entirely student-led: the core components of leadership, design, and execution are decisions made at the student's discretion.
Since our rookie year, we have:
- Two times qualified for the FIRST World Championship.
- Six separate Spirit Awards at the FIRST Midwest Regional. 
- Taught essential engineering and technology skills to all team members.
- Supported other FRC and FLL teams in their team design plan and structure.
- Proud contributor and promoter of CSEdWeek at Lane Tech.
